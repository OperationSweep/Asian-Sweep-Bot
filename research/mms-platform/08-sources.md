## Downtime cost figures

- [Oxmaint — Unplanned Downtime Cost in Power Plants](https://oxmaint.com/industries/power-plant/power-plant-unplanned-downtime-cost)

`[VENDOR]` **All downtime-cost figures in this folder are labelled `[VENDOR]` and should be treated as illustrative only.** They are published by companies selling maintenance software, the ranges quoted ($50k–$500k per hour) are too wide to be a number, and no methodology is given. They are used in `06-pricing.md` §6.3 solely to make an order-of-magnitude framing argument, and explicitly not as the basis for any pricing. If the client needs a real figure, it should come from their own generation margin and outage records — which the MMS itself will eventually produce, and which is the whole point.
