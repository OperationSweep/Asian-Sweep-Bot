# 05 — Sources

## Source-quality tiering

| Tier | Meaning | Weight given |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Primary — the company's own site, official store listings, regulators, BBB records, trademark registries | Treated as fact for what it *says*; company claims about scale are labelled as claims |
| **T2** | Named research houses, established industry publications, panel-data providers | Treated as `[FACT]` where a figure is attributed and dated |
| **T3** | Independent review sites and affiliate-adjacent review content | Used only for **convergent** themes — where several unrelated sites agree — never for a solitary claim |

⚠️ **A warning specific to this subject.** Finelo is a high-payout consumer subscription product, and the "Finelo review" search space is heavily populated by **affiliate content with a financial interest in conversions**. Several sources below are T3 and some are plausibly affiliate-monetised. They are used here only where multiple independent T3 sources converge (notably: "content is too basic for advanced traders" and "complaints are about billing, not fraud"), and never as the sole support for a numeric claim. **Every number in this research traces to T1 or T2.**
