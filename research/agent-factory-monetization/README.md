# The Boss Playbook — 30 Businesses You Can Run on an AI Agent Factory

**Prepared for:** the operator of this repo (the Boss)
**Date:** August 2026 · **Currency:** £GBP · **Base:** United Kingdom

---

## What this document is

You are building an agent factory: swarms of AI agents in isolated cloud sandboxes, each swarm run by an **in-sandbox orchestrator**, with an **out-loop orchestrator** that reports to you every morning and evening. You show up at the beginning (planning, approving) and the end (reading reports, making calls). Nothing in between.

This document is the menu of 30 businesses that architecture can actually run — scored honestly, with the swarm design, the money path, and the human touchpoints named for each one. It was generated by a multi-agent ideation pass (six category specialists plus an adversarial critic) and curated down from 41 candidates. It builds on the two assets already in this repo:

- **`bot.py`** — a working Asian-session liquidity-sweep + BOS trading bot.
- **`research/supplement-ecommerce/`** — a completed UK supplement e-commerce investigation (verdict: MODIFY then GO, £25k, 3 SKUs, subscription-first, TikTok Shop affiliate found first-order profitable where paid social is not).

Plus your connected stack: **Shopify** (full admin + analytics), **Stripe**, **Gmail**, **Google Calendar**, **Motion** (AI video generation), **GitHub**, and Claude Code / Agent SDK skills.

---

## The honesty rules

Every "make money with AI agents" list on the internet fails the same way: it scores fantasy, not operations. This list obeys four rules:

1. **Autonomy is scored 1–10 and honestly.** Physical goods, ad platforms, regulated claims, KYC, and customer trust all create human touchpoints. An honest 6 beats a fantasy 10. Nothing on this list is a 10 in month one.
2. **Every idea names its swarm** — the agent roles, what runs daily, and what the orchestrator reports.
3. **Every idea has a real buyer** and a first-revenue path measured in weeks, not "after 1M followers."
4. **Nothing illegal or deceptive.** No fake reviews, no spam, no impersonation. Regulatory exposure (FCA, ASA/MHRA, GDPR/PECR, platform ToS) is flagged inside each idea's risk line.

## The daily report contract

Whatever business you pick, the out-loop orchestrator's report is the product you are really building. Standardise it from day one, across every venture, so a portfolio of businesses reads like one dashboard:

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| **💰 Money** | Revenue today / MTD vs target · contribution after costs · cash position · spend (ads, API tokens, infra) |
| **📈 Reach** | Sessions / views / opens / impressions by channel · conversion rate · CAC where paid |
| **👥 Customers** | New customers · churn/refunds · support tickets opened/resolved/escalated |
| **📦 Operations** | Inventory accuracy / stock cover (if physical) · pipeline state · agent task completions & failures · QA-gate pass rate |
| **🚨 Bad news** | Anything red, stated plainly, each with a proposed mitigation and what the swarm already did about it |
| **🎯 Tomorrow** | The swarm's plan for the next 24h and any decision that needs YOU (kept to ≤3 items) |

**Non-negotiable design rule:** the report must distinguish *"handled — FYI"* from *"needs your decision."* If everything needs your decision, you built an intern, not a factory.

# Category A — Trading & market data (built on `bot.py`)

### 1. Strategy Foundry ⭐

**Best-of-N strategy research: breed, backtest and paper-trade session-based price-action strategies in parallel sandboxes, promoting only survivors to prop-firm funded capital.**

- **The swarm:** 4 researcher agents (one strategy family each: Asian-sweep variants, London/NY session sweeps, BOS continuation, Asian-range mean-reversion) run daily hypothesis generation and parameter search in separate sandboxes. 1 data-integrity agent (nightly candle/gap/timezone audits — bad data is the top silent killer). 1 adversarial backtest-QA agent hunting lookahead bias and overfitting (walk-forward + out-of-sample mandatory). 1 paper-trade executor running the top 5 on demo accounts 24/5. 1 risk sentinel enforcing hard caps (max daily loss, max position, kill-switch) that no other agent can override. Orchestrator runs the weekly promote/demote tournament.
- **The money:** trading profit on own/funded capital — no customers, no signals sold, so no FCA permission needed. Pass 1–2 prop-firm evaluations (£300–600 each) to control $50–100k funded capital at 80–90% profit split; a modest 2–3%/month on $100k is roughly £1,200–1,800/month.
- **Report KPIs:** live and paper P&L in £ and R-multiples · strategies per tournament stage · max drawdown vs cap per account · data-integrity incidents · overfit rejections · distance to next prop payout.
- **Autonomy 7/10 · £300–800 · first £ in 2–4 months**
- **You still do:** prop-firm KYC and withdrawals; explicit approval for every paper-to-live promotion (never automate this gate); verifying each firm's automated-trading rules in writing before signup.
- **Dies if:** the edge is regime-dependent and dies quietly — great backtest, live bleed. Mitigate with the adversarial QA mandate, a mandatory 6-week paper gate, hard risk caps, and eval fees treated as cheap optionality.
- **Leverages:** the Asian-sweep bot is strategy family #1 and provides the scaffolding every researcher agent inherits.

### 2. Edge Audit ⭐

**Productized strategy stress-testing for retail algo traders: submit a strategy, receive a rigorous robustness report (walk-forward, Monte Carlo, parameter sensitivity) within 72 hours. £199–249.**

- **The swarm:** intake agent (parses the customer's Pine Script/Python/English description into a canonical spec, emails clarifying questions); 2 parallel backtest agents on independent data sources so results cross-check; a bias auditor that proves the tests catch planted lookahead bugs; robustness agent (Monte Carlo, parameter heatmaps, regime splits, slippage stress); report-writer; QA agent that independently re-derives every headline number before release. Orchestrator manages the 72-hour SLA queue.
- **The money:** r/algotrading, prop-firm Discords, fintwit — people about to risk a £500 eval on an untested idea. £199 standard, £499 deep audit, £59/mo re-validation subscription. 10 audits + 15 subs ≈ £2.9k/month at near-zero marginal cost.
- **Report KPIs:** queue depth · delivered vs SLA · QA rejection rate (numbers failing re-derivation, target zero) · revenue mix · refund rate · top acquisition channel.
- **Autonomy 7/10 · £100–300 · first £ in 1–2 weeks**
- **You still do:** review the first ~20 reports until the QA agent earns trust; police scope ruthlessly — the report describes statistics, it never says "trade this" (that line is where FCA advice territory begins).
- **Dies if:** scope creeps into regulated investment advice. Mitigate with a hard-coded descriptive-only template, refusal rules in every agent prompt, and no performance promises in marketing.
- **Leverages:** the bot's backtest and data infrastructure IS the audit engine; your own validation scars are the marketing story.

### 3. SweepStats

**A nightly-updated statistics product for session-based price-action traders: Asian-range behaviour, sweep frequencies, BOS follow-through rates across 25 FX/futures instruments — statistics, not signals. £19/month.**

- **The swarm:** 5 market-coverage agents (5 instruments each: nightly session-range percentiles, sweep-frequency tables, time-of-day distributions); data-QA agent (gap/outlier/DST detection gating all publication); publisher agent (dashboard + weekly PDF digest); content agent (daily three-stat session recap on X + weekly Motion explainer clip); support-triage agent on Stripe/Gmail. Orchestrator halts publishing on any QA failure. Variants: run it as a 6am pre-London email brief, or expose the pipeline as a £29–59/mo data feed/API for algo traders.
- **The money:** the ICT/Smart-Money-Concepts retail crowd demonstrably pays £20–60/month for tools. 100 subscribers = £1,900/month; 300 is a realistic 12-month ceiling. Free delayed one-instrument tier as the funnel.
- **Report KPIs:** MRR and net subscriber change · nightly pipeline success and QA holds · free-to-paid conversion · content reach · tickets auto-resolved vs escalated.
- **Autonomy 8/10 · £50–150 · first £ in 4–6 weeks**
- **You still do:** one-time check of data-vendor redistribution terms (derived aggregates are usually fine, raw data is not); a weekly skim of published content for drift from statistics into "trade this" territory.
- **Dies if:** the content agent implies predictive value ("sweeps resolve up 68% — buy the dip") and it becomes a UK financial promotion. Mitigate with descriptive-only templates and standing disclaimers.
- **Leverages:** the bot already computes most of these statistics — the product largely exposes existing internals. Its event stream is a proprietary dataset that grows every session while competitors post opinions.

### 4. Prop-Firm Radar

- **Dies if:** incentive corruption — recommending a firm that collapses with users' eval fees torches the asset. Mitigate: rubric-driven rankings independent of commission; publish distress warnings even when they cost affiliate revenue.
- **Leverages:** deep domain fluency from funding the bot on these exact firms; Strategy Foundry's prop research is this product's seed content.

### 5. Sweep Bot Pro

**Sell the Asian-sweep bot itself as self-hosted software (not signals): traders run it on their own accounts, backed by a continuously published live paper-trade track record.**

- **The swarm:** regression agent (nightly run against fresh data across all supported pairs — no release ships a silent breakage); onboarding agent (guides each buyer through broker connection by email); per-customer backtest agent (custom report for the buyer's pair at purchase — the unboxing moment); docs agent; marketing-content agent (Motion walkthroughs, transparent monthly paper-results posts); support-triage agent. - **The money:** £199 one-time or £19/month including updates, sold to r/algotrading, ForexFactory and prop communities. 15 sales/month = £3k/month. The trust wedge in a scam-saturated market: a verifiable, continuously published paper track record.
- **Report KPIs:** sales and MRR · nightly regression pass rate · live paper equity curve vs published claims · support auto-resolution rate · refund rate.
- **Autonomy 6/10 · £100–300 · first £ in 6–10 weeks** (the track record needs to run publicly first)

- **Dies if:** a "made 40% last year" claim appears in an ad — that's a regulated promotion, policed reactively but harshly. Mitigate: factual feature-based marketing only, prominent risk warnings, honest published drawdowns.
- **Leverages:** the bot is the product, unchanged at core.

# Sequencing — the first 180 days

The critic's portfolio rule, worth adopting verbatim: **maximum two revenue engines at once, sequenced by (customer count × regulatory surface), ascending. No third concurrent business until one has run 30 days without a single unrequested human intervention.**

- **Days 0–30:** build the orchestrator/report substrate by launching **Strategy Foundry** (cheapest, zero customers — the perfect forcing function). In parallel, launch **Edge Audit** for cash flow; it shares the same backtest engine. Human-only prep for the supplement decision if you want it: RFQs, claims checklist, duplicate ad infrastructure.
- **Days 30–90:** launch **Dose & Evidence Engine** (or, if you're committing capital, **Baseline Stack** with hard budget caps *in orchestrator code* — its known kill risk is cash exhaustion, and agents will spend to zero if the cap lives in a prompt instead of code).
- **Days 90–180:** add ONE B2B service (**StoreWatch** or **Motion Ad Foundry**) sold on the case study your own ventures generated. Both compete for your scarce founder-sales hours — pick one.
- **Day 180+:** ship **ForgeKit/Factory Logs** with 60+ days of real reports as the launch content, then consider **Ghost Crew** to wholesale the proven pods.
