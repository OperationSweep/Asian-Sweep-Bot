## Source-quality note

`[ANALYSIS]` Sources above are not of equal weight, and the research treats them accordingly:

- **Tier 1 (relied on heavily):** company annual reports and results announcements (Glanbia, Applied Nutrition, THG, H&B), Companies House filings, government and regulator sources (HMRC, MHRA, ASA/CAP, GOV.UK), platform policy documentation (Meta Transparency Center, TikTok Ads/Shop policy, Google Ads policy), and the CRN consumer survey.
- **Tier 2 (used with the published caveat):** IBISWorld, Mintel, and named market-research houses. Reported as published, with ranges quoted where houses disagree.
- **Tier 3 (used for direction only, never for modelling):** DTC benchmark aggregators, trade-press trend forecasts, TikTok Shop trend data, and platform-published promotional statistics. These informed judgement; **no financial projection in this research is built on them.**

The financial model in `09-financial-model.md` is built bottom-up from UK unit economics and stated assumptions, not top-down from any Tier 2 or Tier 3 market-size figure.
