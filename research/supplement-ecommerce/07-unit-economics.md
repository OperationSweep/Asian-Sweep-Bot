### The single most important conclusion in this entire research document

**Not one single-unit order in the table above covers even the *Strong-case* £38 CAC.**

The best performer — a greens powder — returns £20.23 against a £38 best-case acquisition cost. The worst — psyllium fibre — returns £5.21. **Selling one product at a time to a cold paid-social audience loses money on every single order, in every scenario, without exception.**

`[ANALYSIS]` This is not a pessimistic reading; it is the arithmetic, and it is why most faceless supplement stores quietly die at 4–8 months with an empty ad account and a garage full of stock. There are exactly **three** ways out, and a viable business needs all three:

1. **Raise AOV through bundles** (Phase 11) — takes contribution from ~£14 to ~£33 per order
2. **Convert to subscription** — takes one £14 order and turns it into £70–£120 of annual contribution
3. **Reduce CAC below the benchmark** — via TikTok Shop affiliate (commission replaces upfront ad spend), organic content, and email/SMS

Any plan missing one of these three is not a plan.

| Payback period | Never | **~12 months** | **~5 months** |

**`[ANALYSIS]` Read this table carefully — it is the investment case in miniature.**

- **Conservative case is not a "slow start". It is a business that destroys capital and never recovers.** Every £1,000 of ad spend returns ~£420 of lifetime contribution. No amount of scale fixes it.
- **The Realistic base case breaks even at exactly 12 months and only becomes properly profitable in year two.** This is the honest centre of gravity for a new UK supplement brand on paid social, and it is consistent with the published benchmark that supplement subscription LTV of $300–£600 accrues over *multiple years*, not one `[FACT]`. It means **the business is a 24-month capital commitment, not a 6-month one.** Anyone modelling a payback inside two quarters is wrong.
- **Strong case is a genuinely good business** — 4.76:1 at 24 months and a 5-month payback. But it requires a working creative engine, 32% subscription attach and a £38 CAC simultaneously. That is achievable; it is not achievable in month one.

**The honest framing: this business is a bet that you can move from the Realistic to the Strong column within 9–12 months, while being funded through a 12-month payback period.** That transition is driven almost entirely by (a) subscription attach rate, (b) AOV via bundles, and (c) creative efficiency. It is **not** driven by product selection — the products are fine. Choosing better products cannot save bad retention; good retention can survive mediocre products.

# PHASE 11 — Bundling

## Why bundling is the load-bearing wall

`[DERIVED]` Comparing like for like:

| | Single unit (creatine) | Foundation Stack (3 products) |
|---|---|---|
| Retail | £29.99 | £64.99 |
| COGS | £3.20 | £10.50 |
| Fulfilment + shipping | £5.50 | £7.10 |
| **Contribution before CAC** | **£14.57** | **£33.44** |
| vs £55 CAC | **−£40.43** | **−£21.56** |
| vs £38 CAC | **−£23.43** | **−£4.56** |

**One bundle sale is worth 2.3 single-unit sales, at almost identical fulfilment cost.** Bundling is the cheapest available lever — it requires no extra ad spend, no extra CAC and no new customers. **Every landing page should default to a bundle, with the single unit as the downgrade option, not the other way round.**

## Responsible-formulation rules `[ANALYSIS]` — what we will NOT bundle

The brief said "do not combine ingredients irresponsibly". Explicit rules:

1. **Never two caffeine sources in one stack** (pre-workout + a caffeinated greens/energy product). Cap total caffeine per stack at 200mg/day and state it on-pack.
2. **Never iron in a general/male-default stack.** Iron is included only in the Women's Foundation stack, with clear guidance. Iron overload is a genuine harm and iron is a leading cause of paediatric poisoning — this is also why the "keep out of reach of children" warning matters.
3. **Do not co-dose iron and calcium** (absorption antagonism) — separate them across morning/evening in the routine guidance.
4. **Do not stack multiple products containing the same fat-soluble vitamin** (A, D, E, K) without checking the combined total against safe upper levels. Our Everyday Essentials and Healthy Ageing stacks both contain D3 — **they must never be sold as a combined "double stack"** without reformulation.
5. **No stack combines more than one adaptogen at full clinical dose** without an interaction review.
6. **Every multi-product stack ships with a printed insert** stating total daily amounts of each nutrient across the whole stack, and the "do not exceed" warning at stack level, not just per product.
7. **No stack is marketed to a condition** — no "PCOS bundle", no "menopause bundle", no "IBS bundle", regardless of how well they would sell. See `05-uk-regulation.md` §7.1.
8. **Pregnancy/breastfeeding and medication-interaction warnings** on ashwagandha, high-dose vitamin A, and anything with meaningful pharmacological activity.

## AOV impact modelling

| Mix scenario | Singles | Bundles | Blended AOV | Blended contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No bundle strategy | 100% | 0% | £28 | £12.90 |
| **Weak execution** | 70% | 30% | £39 | £20.10 |
| **Base case target** | 45% | 55% | £48 | £27.36 |
| **Strong execution** | 30% | 70% | £58 | £34.10 |

**`[ANALYSIS]`** Moving bundle mix from 30% to 70% lifts contribution per order by **70%** with zero additional ad spend. This is the highest-leverage operational metric in the business and should be the single number on the daily dashboard. Mechanisms: bundle-first landing pages, "complete the stack" upsell at cart, post-purchase one-click upsell, subscribe-and-save applied to bundles only, and free UK shipping above £45 (which sits just above the Starter Bundle and just below the Foundation Stack — deliberately).
