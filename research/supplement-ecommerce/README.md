## Executive summary — the eight findings that matter

1. **The model as briefed does not work.** Faceless social + 50 products + single-product selling is arithmetically loss-making. `[DERIVED]` **No single-unit order in the Top 10 covers even a best-case £38 CAC.** The best product returns £20.23 of contribution; the worst returns £5.21. Supplements have the **highest CAC of any DTC category (~$89 ≈ £70)** `[FACT]`.

2. **Bundles and subscription are not optimisations — they are the business model.** One Foundation Stack sale (£64.99) delivers £33.44 of contribution versus £14.57 for a single creatine tub, at almost identical fulfilment cost. Moving bundle mix from 30% to 70% lifts contribution per order by 70% with zero extra ad spend.

3. **Creatine is the standout product opportunity in the world right now.** `[FACT]` **26.2% global CAGR** (28.3% in the US) — the fastest of any category researched — with £3–£4 COGS, a register-authorised health claim, and an audience broadening from gym men to **women, over-45s and cognitive/healthy-ageing users**. Every incumbent still markets it as a commodity add-on to 18–34 men. **That gap is the business.**

4. **The UK online supplement channel is growing at 1% a year, not 10%.** `[FACT]` IBISWorld: **£1.5bn, +1.0% in 2025-26**. There is no rising tide. Every pound must be taken from an incumbent. Global "$200bn market" figures are commercially useless here.

5. **This is a high-gross-margin, low-net-margin industry, and no cleverness changes that.** `[FACT]` Huel makes **7.6% net at £254m**. Glanbia's Performance Nutrition EBITDA margin **fell 380bps** on whey inflation. Applied Nutrition runs 46.7% GM *with its own factory*. `[DERIVED]` **£1m of revenue produces ~£58k of EBITDA.**

6. **Therefore: build for an asset, not an income.** `[FACT]` Huel sold to Danone for ~€1bn (~3.9× revenue); AG1 reached ~$600m on effectively one SKU; Applied Nutrition listed on the LSE at £107m. **A high-retention subscription brand is worth far more sold than milked.** If the goal is monthly income, do something else.

7. **Compliance is the moat, not the overhead.** ~£5k/year buys you something most TikTok-first competitors don't have. And the key regulatory insight is counter-intuitive: **your ad copy, not your formula, is what can reclassify your product as an unlicensed medicine** under MHRA Guidance Note 8.

8. **Peak working capital is ~£80,000 if you run the plan at full speed** — 3–8× what most founders budget. The fix is not more money; it is a **self-funding ramp** (ad spend capped at ≤130% of trailing 30-day contribution), which converts a solvency risk into an 18–24 month timeline.

## What to launch

**Three SKUs, sold as a stack.**

| SKU | Retail | COGS | Contribution/unit | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creatine Monohydrate 500g (Creapure®) | £29.99 | £3.20 | £14.57 | The hero |
| Electrolytes, 30 sticks | £26.99 | £4.50 | £10.88 | The subscription engine |
| Magnesium Glycinate 120 caps | £22.99 | £2.80 | £9.39 | The volume entry point |
| **Foundation Stack** (all three) | **£64.99** | **£10.50** | **£33.44** | **The default offer** |
| **Starter Bundle** (creatine 250g + 15 sticks) | £34.99 | £4.60 | £24.56 | Cold-traffic entry |

**Opening inventory: ~£9,400.**

**Positioning:** *"Foundational performance nutrition, evidence-first, for adults who intend to be strong at 60."*

**Avoid entirely:** weight loss, fat burners, detox (prohibited on TikTok Shop / restricted on Meta) · testosterone and sexual performance (prohibited) · melatonin and DHEA (**POM in GB — illegal to sell OTC**) · CBD and NMN (Novel Foods) · shilajit (heavy metals) · berberine (medicinal-claim territory) · whey protein at launch (WPC +108%, WPI +139%) · generic multivitamins · **dropshipping (scored 22/100 — arithmetically impossible at a £70 CAC)**.

## Evidence labelling

Every material figure in this research carries a label:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[FACT]` | Company filing, regulator, government source, or a named research house's published figure |
| `[DERIVED]` | Calculated from `[FACT]` inputs — the arithmetic is shown |
| `[ESTIMATE]` | A defensible market estimate with stated reasoning |
| `[ASSUMPTION]` | A planning input chosen by me; change it and the model changes |
| `[ANALYSIS]` | My commercial judgement |
| `[NOT PUBLIC]` | No adequate evidence exists — deliberately left unfilled |

**On the critical data rule:** product-level revenue and margin **do not exist as public data** for essentially any supplement ingredient. Public companies report at group or segment level. This research does not disguise company-wide revenue as revenue from a single supplement. The one place a genuine brand-level figure was derivable — Optimum Nutrition, because Glanbia disclosed it represented 75% of Performance Nutrition revenue — is shown with its derivation. Everywhere else, the honest answer is `[NOT PUBLIC]`, and that is what is recorded.
