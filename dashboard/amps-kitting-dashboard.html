Serial lookup

Find any GFF serial: its status, consumption date, and the candidate work orders from that day (exact order pairing needs the serial↔material-document link — SER03/OBJK query).

Rules built in (so you can defend the numbers)
  • Serial status ESTO = in stock; AVLB = consumed (ZPRO'd); ECUS = at customer.
  • For consumed serials, IQ09 "Changed on" = the ZPRO posting date (validated: daily counts reconcile exactly with COOIS net 261s).
  • Movement 261 = issue to order (+1), 262 = reversal (−1). One kitting = one material document = 2 GFFs.
  • GFF material codes are contract-unique; the contract name is read from the IQ09 description.
  • A serial only keeps its last change date, so a reversal moves it to the re-issue day.
