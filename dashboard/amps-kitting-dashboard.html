Rules built in (so you can defend the numbers)
  • 1 work order = 1 AMP. A repeater needs FP-count AMPs (24FP repeater = 24 AMP orders).
  • Planned AMPs per week = ASP weekly repeater quantity × fibre pairs (FP parsed from ITEM_ID, e.g. R512.24REPOPT).
  • An order counts as kitted on its first net-positive GFF issue (261 minus 262) — the ZPRO posting date.
  • Movement 101 on the 92YAF… assembly = AMP built (102 = reversal).
  • Contract = last 3 digits of the 5-digit group in assembly/RRA codes; ASP links via PROD_ID digits.
  • GFF variants are contract-unique; the variant→contract mapping is learned from orders where both appear.
  • Serial statuses (IQ09): ESTO = stock, AVLB = consumed, ECUS = at customer; "Changed on" = consumption date.
